About the Journal

ISSN: 2395-5627 | Frequency: Annual | Publisher: Nabadwip Vidyasagar College

Teachers' Journal is a multi-disciplinary peer-reviewed research journal published annually by Nabadwip Vidyasagar College, Nabadwip, Nadia, West Bengal. It publishes original research papers, review articles and case studies from various disciplines, with an aim to exchange ideas and information and to disseminate diverse knowledge on a wider scale. It illuminates unexplored vistas of fundamental research in a socially and academically beneficial way. Read more
