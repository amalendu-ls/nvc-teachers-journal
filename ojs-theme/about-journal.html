Aims and Scope

Teachers’ Journal (ISSN 2395-5627) is an annual, multilingual, multidisciplinary, peer-reviewed academic journal published by Nabadwip Vidyasagar College, Nabadwip, Nadia, West Bengal, India. The journal is committed to promoting scholarly research across academic disciplines and to encouraging academic discourse among academicians, researchers and intellectuals worldwide.

The journal aims to serve as a vibrant medium for scholarly expression, rooted in academic excellence and global accessibility, with a special focus on promoting regional research in a global context. It illuminates unexplored vistas and horizons of fundamental research in a socially and academically beneficial manner.

What We Publish

Original, unpublished research papers, case studies, scholarly articles and book reviews from the humanities, social sciences, sciences, commerce and allied disciplines.

Languages

Submissions are accepted in English, Bengali, Sanskrit and other recognised languages that meet scholarly publishing standards.

Peer Review

All submissions undergo a blind peer review process to ensure academic quality and integrity. The editors and reviewers may accept, ask for modification of, or reject a manuscript.

Publication Frequency

The journal is published once a year. Each volume is released online on this website and in print.

Open Access

All articles are freely available to read and download immediately on publication. Articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

Responsibility of Authors

All research articles published in this journal are original contributions of their authors, who are solely responsible for the views and content of their articles and for any plagiarism or copyright violations.
